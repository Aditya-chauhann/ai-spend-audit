# Unit Economics

## Value of a Converted Lead to Credex
A converted lead (high savings user) is worth **$1,200–$3,000** in first year revenue. 
Reasoning: Average AI spend $800–2000/mo → 30% savings via Credex credits → $300+/mo per customer. With 40% retention, LTV is high.

## CAC Estimates
- Organic X / Hacker News: ~$0–5
- Warm intros / network: ~$0
- Content + SEO (long term): $15–30

## Conversion Rates Needed
- Audit completed → Email capture: 25–35%
- Email capture → Consultation booked: 12–18%
- Consultation → Credit purchase: 35–45%

**Break-even** at ~8–10% overall audit-to-customer conversion.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 months
- Month 1–3: 200 audits/mo → 25 customers
- Month 6: 1,500 audits/mo → 120 customers
- Month 12: 4,000 audits/mo → 350+ active customers
- At $400 ARPU → $1.68M ARR

**Key Assumption**: Viral coefficient from shareable reports + Credex brand trust makes this realistic.
