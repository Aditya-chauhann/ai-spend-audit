# GTM Strategy

## Target User
Engineering Manager or CTO at Series A/B startups (10-50 engineers) who are scaling AI usage but feel the monthly bill is getting out of control.

## Where they are right before wanting this tool
- Looking at their Claude / Cursor / OpenAI bill at the end of the month
- Complaining in #eng channel about AI costs
- Searching "how much should I spend on Cursor" or "Cursor vs Copilot cost"

## Where they hang out
- r/startups, r/MachineLearning
- Indie Hackers Slack / community
- X lists of startup founders & engineering leaders
- YC Startup School Slack

## First 100 users in 30 days ($0 budget)
1. Post a detailed thread on X with before/after screenshots (target 5-10k impressions)
2. Share in 5 relevant Indie Hackers / YC threads
3. DM 30 engineering managers I know from college/network with personalized message
4. Write a Twitter thread + post on Hacker News ("I built a Mint for AI spend")
5. Ask friends running startups to try and share internally

## Unfair Distribution Channel
Credex's existing customer list + discounted credits offer. Only Credex can offer "we saw you are overspending $800/mo, here's how to cut it in half with our credits."

## Week-1 Traction Goal
- 500 visitors
- 150 audits completed
- 40 email signups
- 8 consultation bookings
